GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I check my queue without staying at reception?

You can check your queue status through My Queue. Follow the clinic's instructions about where to wait.

Why does my waiting position change?

Your position updates as the clinic serves patients. The estimated waiting time may also change.

What if I cannot access my patient information?

Check that you are using the correct portal page. If the problem continues, ask the clinic reception team for assistance.
